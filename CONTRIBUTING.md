### tldr

Contributions from people who __use evo__ or have a __genuine interest__ in the project are welcome.

### Guidelines

__Opening a PR doesn't guarantee it gets merged.__
> If you're unsure, better open an issue to discuss your idea before starting work on a PR.

__Make sure others can understand _why_ you open a PR.__
> Non-trivial PRs without any description can be met with equal silence and simply get closed 🤷‍♂️

### AI

Feel free to use AI assistance! But make sure…
* you have a clear motivation for the change
* you do a human self-review and understand the content & code
* you can discuss it without letting your LLM speak for you

Disclose if your contribution is entirely AI-generated.

### Spam 💩

PRs or issues opened by automations or bots may get closed without comment.

> If you have no real use case for this project and just try to do contribution farming for your CV:
>
> You're wasting your tokens and other people's time, plus it improves none of your skills.
A single high-quality contribution in a project _you actually care about_ would bring you further than spamming random repos.
